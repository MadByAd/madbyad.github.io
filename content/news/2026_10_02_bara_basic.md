Bara Basic in Arabic and Learning is a horror comedy game set in an arabic schoolhouse where you and your friends have to collect notebook and solve arabic quizzes while being chased by Bara the Arabic teacher. It is a local multiplayer game which means to play with your friends you need to be on the same WIFI or on LAN. The game is only available on [Itch.io](https://madbyad.itch.io/bara-basic)
