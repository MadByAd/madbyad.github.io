## About The Game

You just got a new job, congratulations! It is a corporate lifestyle! you now work in a office doing corporate stuff on night time. But something feels off, something isn't right your employer said that an employee just got possesed by a demon or something, you try to brush it off that night but it doesn't quite get away, you know there something. The night seems to be really quite, are you alone? or not?

Scary Corporate Life Simulator, in this game you work as an employee doing corporate stuff working for a financing company. You work on a nightshift and your job is to input data to a spreadsheet. Recently another employee just got possesed and has been seen roaming around the office even though he wasn't supposed to be there. Can you finish your job and survive?

Use CCTV to monitor the building and a flashlight to scare away any intruder! The power is oddly unstable so don't forget to keep that in mind and don't try not to make mistake when inputting data! And incase something went wrong, luckily the desk are big enough for you to hide underneath!
