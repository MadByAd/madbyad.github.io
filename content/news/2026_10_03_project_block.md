Project Block is our newest game currently in development, exploring the idea of a multiplayer sandbox game built around a voxel-based world.

The project is still in its very early stages. Right now, there isn't much to show in terms of gameplay. Most of our work has been focused on the foundations of the game, including the voxel engine, world systems, and basic multiplayer functionality.

While the project is still a long way from becoming a complete game, we already have a direction in mind for where we want to take it. The goal is to gradually build a persistent, open-ended world where players can explore, build, and interact with each other.

For now, we're focusing on getting the fundamentals right before moving on to more visible gameplay features. There is a lot to figure out, but this is where Project Block begins.

**Note:** Project Block isn't the official nor final game name yet and may subject to changes in the future
