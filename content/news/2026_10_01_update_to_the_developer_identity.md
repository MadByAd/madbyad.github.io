As of October 2026, we've decided that it was the time for MadByAd to take on a new and much more polished identity

As we were developing new games and exploring bigger ideas, we felt that our website and developer identity should match with this new vision of ours. The previous design was good but it doesn't fit with where we want MadByAd to go, so we decided that it is time for a redesign of the developer identity.

The redesign is more than just a logo update. With a new and much more looking logo of ours, we also updated our website and brand design to better reflect of what we are going to do next. The new website is now much more suited for our next new catalog of games we are working on.

Part of this decision also comes from a new project that we are working one. It is much bigger than the usual project we haave worked before, and we wanted the MadByAd developer identity and website to feel right with this new project of ours.

There is still a lot of work ahead, but we're excited about where this direction will take us. This website will also become a place where we can share development updates, news, and stories from our projects along the way.
