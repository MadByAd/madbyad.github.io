About MadByAd

MadByAd is a small independent game studio crafting games since 2024. We create unique and memorable experiences, exploring ideas that go beyond conventional game design while keeping our games fun and engaging.

We believe game development is a space for experimentation. Through our projects, we explore new ideas, creative approaches, and different ways of using technology to create interactive experiences. We are not afraid to experiment and iterate to see how far we can push the boundaries of what games can be.

At MadByAd, we see every game as an opportunity to learn, create, and explore. Whether it is through gameplay, visuals, technology, or storytelling, we strive to build games that reflect our curiosity and creativity while offering players something unique to experience.
